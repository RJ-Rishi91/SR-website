Scoping & Investment

Protocol 01

High-performance custom architecture is not an off-the-shelf commodity. Every enterprise platform, custom headless flagship, and interactive web application demands an exact evaluation of user state transitions, complex API orchestration, animations, and targeted commercial KPIs.

Standard rate cards inevitably introduce bloat or force corners to be cut. By calibrating our engagements to precise technical requirements, we engineer lean, bespoke milestone agreements with zero superfluous overhead. We deliver complete, custom-architected proposals within 24 to 48 hours of technical intake.

bolt Every proposal includes fixed milestone benchmarks, unambiguous delivery deliverables, and zero hidden scope surprises.

Our intake path is structured around three precise milestones:

Phase 01

Discovery Intake

Submit your objectives, architectural prerequisites, and target launch window via our guided form.

Phase 02

30-Min Blueprint Call

A direct architecture deep dive with a principal partner—not an account rep or sales coordinator.

Phase 03

Milestone Blueprint

A line-by-line interactive scope blueprint detailing velocity, technical stacks, and deliverables.

To accelerate project velocity, we conduct a rapid inventory of your existing digital ecosystem during kickoff:

  • check_circle Brand guidelines, typography licenses, logomarks, and existing design systems.
  • check_circle Access to GitHub/GitLab repositories, staging environments, or current API endpoints (if re-platforming).
  • check_circle Identified primary stakeholders empowered to approve sprint deliverables within our 48-hour cadence.
