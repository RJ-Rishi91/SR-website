The Problem Space

Visual opacity in high-velocity financial and telemetry data.

Synthesis possessed cutting-edge streaming algorithms capable of parsing tens of thousands of telemetry data points per second. Yet, their commercial interface was burdened by legacy enterprise clutter: nested table trees, sluggish canvas re-renders, and visual dissonance that obscured high-value strategic signals.

During crucial enterprise sales demos, prospective Fortune 500 buyers hesitated. The user interface communicated prototype instability rather than enterprise stability, extending sales cycles past nine months and driving unacceptable pilot drop-off rates.
